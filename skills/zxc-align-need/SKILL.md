---
name: zxc-align-need
description: Identify the user's needs, provide alignment with goals and available solutions and provide actionable recommendations.
---

## Input
The input is the content provided by the user, which may include:
- A text description of the user's needs
- A code snippet
- A question or task description
- Other forms of expressing needs

## Responsibility
Analyze the user's input to identify needs and align them with available solutions.

1. Receive and review the user's input.
2. Analyze the user's input to identify needs.
3. Explain the understanding of the identified needs.
4. Align the user's needs with goals and available solutions or resources and propose feasible approaches.
5. Generate specific actionable recommendations or solutions based on the alignment results.
6. Provide the generated recommendations or solutions back to the user.
7. **Just give Output without task executing**.

## Output
The output is the result of analyzing and aligning the user's input, which includes:
1. Understanding of the user's needs(usually means identifying the user's goals)
2. Feasible approaches
3. Specific actionable recommendations or solutions
4. Feedback to the user or further clarification of needs
